05 / GOOD QUESTIONS

Let's clear
a few things up.

Do I need a contract or monthly plan?+

A clear project agreement records your pages, deliverables, payment milestones, rights and what happens if you stop the build. There is no mandatory monthly plan. Optional monthly services have no minimum term and can be cancelled before their next renewal.

Does the website really belong to me?+

Yes. Once your build is fully paid, we hand over the site and assign the rights we hold in work created specifically for you, in writing. Third-party components keep their disclosed licences. Your domain and accounts remain in your name, and you keep admin access.

What if I leave after one month?+

You keep your paid-for website. Cancel optional monthly help before its next renewal and it ends at the paid period's end. We include the standard handover/export without an agency exit fee. Arrange replacement hosting before your current hosting ends. Third-party services keep their own billing terms.

Are domain, hosting and email included?+

Build prices cover the build. Your domain and email are itemised separately in your name. Website Care includes hosting for one supported brochure site. Otherwise you choose hosting separately. Shopify includes store hosting in its own subscription, which you pay directly; business email is separate.

What if I need booking, membership or something custom?+

We check the integration and costs before quoting. Extra features, advanced animation, complex forms, international stores and large migrations require an agreed scope. You see any software subscription and licence restrictions before approval.

Will I rank first or get guaranteed leads?+

We can improve pages, search foundations and campaigns, then report the work and results. Competition, demand, location, budget and your offer all matter. Specific rankings, lead volumes and financial returns cannot be guaranteed.

How is my Google Ads budget handled?+

Your advertising spend is paid directly to Google, separately from our setup and management fees. You retain account access. We agree the budget before launch and get approval before increasing it. Google's daily spend can fluctuate; we explain the applicable billing limits before campaigns start.

Can I move a Shopify store to any host?+

You can change agency and keep your store, domain, account and history. Shopify is a hosted platform: its subscription continues while your shop operates there. Moving to a different platform can require a rebuild, and exports don't include every setting or app feature.
